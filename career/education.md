### Bachelor's degree, School of Mechanical Engineering, College of Engineering, 2014–2020

#### Undergraduate capstone

This capstone continued the robotic-arm remote-control project I worked on during my laboratory internship. A public [STM32-based orientation-estimation implementation](https://github.com/hhk7734/stm32_ahrs/tree/f7176e118cd2a225f6c4c69fb6192757059d70d1) uses an MPU9250 sensor, quaternion-based extended Kalman filtering, and nRF24 wireless transmission. Companion MATLAB tooling converted streamed quaternion data into roll, pitch, and yaw.

**Advisor:** Prof. Jong-Hyeon Park, Department of Mechanical Engineering.
